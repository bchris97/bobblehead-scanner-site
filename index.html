Q & A

Frequently asked questions

How do I find out what my bobblehead is worth?

Take a photo of it with Bobblehead Scanner. The app identifies the player, team, year and edition, then searches live eBay listings for that exact item and shows you the range sellers are currently asking.

Where do the values come from?

Values come from current eBay listings, which means they reflect asking prices rather than completed sales. They are a live read on what sellers want for the item today, not an appraisal.

Are stadium giveaway (SGA) bobbleheads worth anything?

Most are not, because teams typically hand out 15,000 to 40,000 of them in a single night. The exceptions are the ones that matter: early giveaways, Hall of Fame players, short-run promotions, and anything still sealed in the original box. Bobblehead Scanner flags a scan as a stadium giveaway so you know which category you are in.

Is Bobblehead Scanner free?

Your first 5 scans are free, with no account and no sign-up. Bobblehead Scanner Pro unlocks unlimited scans, unlimited collection storage, collection value tracking, and automatic cloud backup for $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year.

Does it work on vintage bobbleheads and nodders?

Yes. Vintage papier-mâché nodders, modern stadium giveaways, mascots and limited editions all scan the same way. Older and rarer pieces are harder to identify confidently, so photograph the base and any nameplate text clearly for the best result.

Is my collection private?

There is no account and no sign-up. Your collection and its photos live on your device. Scan photos are sent over an encrypted connection only to identify the item and are never stored on our servers. Pro subscribers get an encrypted cloud backup of their collection details, which never includes photos. Read the full privacy policy.
