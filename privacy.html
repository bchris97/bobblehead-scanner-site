Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

Bobblehead Scanner is built to collect as little about you as possible. The app requires no account, and we never ask for your name, email address, or payment details.

Photos you scan

When you scan a bobblehead, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to Anthropic (our AI provider) to identify the item. Photos are processed in memory and are not stored on our servers. Per Anthropic's commercial API terms, content submitted through the API is not used to train their models. Photo thumbnails shown in your collection are stored only on your device.

Your collection

Your collection — items, values, notes, and photos — is stored on your device. If you subscribe to Pro, the item details in your collection (names, teams, years, values, and notes, but never your photos) are also backed up over an encrypted connection to our server, so your collection can be restored if you reinstall the app or switch to a new phone. The backup is associated with the same random, anonymous identifier used for subscription management and is not linked to your identity.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status using a random, anonymous identifier generated on your device. We do not receive your name, email address, or any payment information.

Scan usage

To enforce free and Pro scan limits, our server stores a count of scans associated with the same anonymous identifier. This identifier is not linked to your identity.

Market pricing

To estimate an item's value, our server sends item search terms (for example, a player name and year) to eBay's API. Your photos and your identity are never shared with eBay.

Analytics and advertising

The app shows no ads, contains no third-party analytics SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

We do advertise Bobblehead Scanner on the App Store using Apple Ads. If you installed the app after tapping one of those ads, the app asks Apple's AdServices framework for an attribution token and passes it to RevenueCat, which exchanges it with Apple for the campaign, ad group, and keyword that led to the install. That information is stored against the same random, anonymous identifier used for subscription management, so we can tell which ads actually lead to subscriptions. It is not linked to your identity.

This relies on Apple's own first-party attribution. It does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and it does not combine your data with data collected from other companies' apps or websites — which is why the app does not ask you for tracking permission.

Children

Bobblehead Scanner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your device. If you subscribed to Pro, your cloud collection backup remains on our server so it can be restored after a reinstall. To request deletion of your cloud backup or the server-side scan counts associated with your device, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
