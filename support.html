Help with Bobblehead Scanner

How scanning works

Point the camera at your bobblehead so it fills the frame, then tap the shutter. The app identifies the item and looks up its market value from recent eBay sales. Good lighting and a clear view of the figure (including any nameplate or base text) give the best results.

Free scans and Pro

Your first 5 scans are free. Bobblehead Scanner Pro ($4.99/month or $29.99/year) unlocks unlimited scans, live eBay market pricing, unlimited collection storage, and collection value tracking.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID.

Canceling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, select Bobblehead Scanner, and tap Cancel. You keep Pro access until the end of the paid period.

A scan came back wrong

Identification is AI-powered and occasionally misses — especially for rare or custom items. Try rescanning with better lighting and the nameplate visible. Values are estimates based on recent sales and can vary.
